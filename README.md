## Featured work

**[n8n-content-pipeline](https://github.com/Alvi-808/n8n-content-pipeline)**: An import-ready n8n workflow that generates an SEO article and survives a primary LLM outage by failing over to a second provider (Groq to Gemini). Runs end to end with zero credentials, so you can import it and press Execute in about five minutes.

**[carousel-renderer](https://github.com/Alvi-808/carousel-renderer)**: A standalone tool that turns structured content into branded carousel and slide decks, exported to PNG and PDF through headless Chrome. Bring your own theme and content.

**[graph-rag-demo](https://github.com/Alvi-808/graph-rag-demo)**: A compact demonstration of graph-RAG over a codebase: parse source with the AST, build a knowledge graph of symbols and dependencies, then answer structural questions retrieval-first instead of grepping raw text.
